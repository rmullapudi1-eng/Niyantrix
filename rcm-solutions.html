The Lifecycle

Full-Cycle RCM — Every Step Optimized

Niyantrix manages the complete revenue cycle — from the moment a patient is scheduled to the day the final payment posts.

🔍

Eligibility & Benefits Verification

Real-time eligibility checks against 2,000+ payers before every patient encounter. Catches inactive coverage, incorrect plan details, and benefit limitations before they become denials.

📋

Charge Capture & Medical Coding

Certified coders supported by AI review every encounter for accurate CPT, ICD-10, and modifier assignment. Reduces undercoding and ensures maximum legitimate reimbursement.

⚡

AI Claim Scrubbing & Submission

Every claim passes through 300+ payer-specific edit rules before submission. Our 98% first-pass acceptance rate eliminates costly rework cycles and accelerates cash flow.

💳

Payment Posting & Reconciliation

Automated ERA/EOB processing with intelligent variance detection. Every underpayment, contractual adjustment, and posting error is flagged for review — capturing revenue others miss.

🚫

Denial Management & Appeals

Root-cause analysis of every denial, automated resubmission workflows, and formal appeals with clinical documentation support. Our denial overturn rate exceeds 72%.

📊

Analytics & Reporting Dashboard

Real-time KPI dashboards covering AR aging, payer performance, denial trends, and collection rates — giving you full visibility and control over your financial health.
