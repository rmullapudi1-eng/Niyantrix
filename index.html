FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About RCM

What is revenue cycle management and why does it matter?

+

Revenue cycle management (RCM) is the end-to-end financial process that healthcare organizations use to manage clinical and administrative functions associated with claims processing, payment, and revenue generation. Effective RCM directly impacts cash flow, reduces administrative burden, and ensures your organization is reimbursed for every service rendered.

How does AI improve revenue cycle management outcomes?

+

AI improves RCM by automating repetitive tasks like claim scrubbing and eligibility verification, predicting claim denials before submission, and identifying patterns in payer behavior that human analysts would miss. The result is a higher clean claim rate, fewer denials, faster reimbursements, and more revenue with less administrative staff time.

What types of healthcare organizations does Niyantrix serve?

+

Niyantrix serves a wide range of healthcare organizations including independent physician practices, multi-specialty groups, ambulatory surgical centers, hospitals, behavioral health providers, and specialty clinics. Our scalable platform adapts to organizations of every size — from solo practitioners to large health systems.

How quickly can Niyantrix improve our AR collections?

+

Most clients see measurable improvement in AR days and collection rates within the first 60–90 days of onboarding. Our initial revenue audit identifies the highest-impact opportunities immediately, allowing our team to begin recovering aged claims and improving the submission process from day one.
